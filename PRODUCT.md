# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Founders and product leads at funded/early-stage startups evaluating an agency to design and build their product. They're comparing portfolios and want to feel the agency is technically sharp and won't produce something generic.

## Product Purpose

arcaniStudio's marketing site (Astro + Tailwind v4 + React islands). Sells design/dev services to startups, drives contact/quote conversions. Success = visitors trust the craft enough to reach out.

## Brand Personality

Warm, editorial, crafted. Confident and premium, not corporate. Typographic and considered rather than loud or templated. Voice: direct, specific, no buzzwords.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS template look (cream/off-white body bg, gradient text, hero-metric blocks, tiny uppercase eyebrows on every section).
- Corporate agency cliches (stock photography, identical 3-card icon grids, numbered 01/02/03 scaffolding on every section, side-stripe accent borders).

## Design Principles

- Editorial restraint: typography and spacing carry hierarchy, not decoration.
- One accent color (brand orange, `--color-primary-500`), used deliberately.
- Break the centered/symmetric grid reflex; favor asymmetric, considered layouts.
- Specific, plain copy — no marketing buzzwords, no aphoristic cadence.
- Dark (`palette-950`) and light (`background-light`) sections both in use; keep transitions intentional, not abrupt.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA. Body text ≥4.5:1 contrast, large text ≥3:1. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for all animations/transitions.
