Design principles

  1. Editorial restraint. Typography and spacing carry the hierarchy — not decoration.
  2. One accent. Brand orange, used deliberately, once per view.
  3. Break the centre reflex. Asymmetric and considered beats symmetric and safe.
  4. Plain, specific copy. Numbers over adjectives. No buzzwords.
  5. Intentional light and dark. Both surfaces are in use; the transition between them is a decision, not an accident.

arcaniStudio — Brand Guidelines 03 · Voice in practice

Say the specific thing

Left column is live site copy. Right column is what the same idea sounds like when it goes generic.

✓ Write this

“A landing page ships in 7–10 business days, a full website in 3–5 weeks.”

✓ Write this

“Book a 30-minute call. No pitch decks, no commitment — just the fastest path from idea to launch.”

✓ Write this

“On final payment, the code, designs and data belong to you. No license traps.”

✓ Write this

“We dig into your users, competitors and constraints before touching a pixel.”

✕ Not this

“Lightning-fast turnaround times tailored to your needs.”

✕ Not this

“Let's connect and explore how we can synergize on your digital transformation journey.”

✕ Not this

“We offer industry-leading, client-first ownership terms.”

✕ Not this

“Our proven, holistic discovery framework unlocks your true potential.”

Rule of thumb

If a competitor could paste the sentence onto their own site unchanged, it isn't ours. Rewrite it with a number, a name, or a constraint.

Sentence length

Short first sentence. Then one longer one that carries the detail. Em dashes for the aside, not semicolons.

Bilingual

English and French are peers, not translations. French copy is written for French readers — same shape, same key count, never machine-translated.

arcaniStudio — Brand Guidelines 04 · The mark

One path, four quadrants

A pinwheel cut from a 256 × 256 square. Each quadrant is a square with one corner carved by an arc — the carve alternates direction, so the shape turns without ever rotating.

Construction — 256 unit artboard, arc radii r100 and r128 struck from centre.

The mark — primary lockup, ink on cream.

arcaniStudio — Brand Guidelines 05 · Clear space & size

Give it room

The mark is dense and geometric. It reads badly when something else crowds the carved corners.

Clear space = ¼ of the mark width

At the 256 artboard that's 64 units on every side. Nothing enters that band — no type, no rule, no photo edge, no other logo. The favicon already bakes this in: its viewBox is -32 -32 320 320, a 12.5% inset for small-size legibility.

arcaniStudio — Brand Guidelines 06 · On backgrounds

The mark takes the colour of the text

Because it's filled with currentColor, there is no separate "white version" file to keep in sync. Set the text colour and the mark follows.

Primary. Ink #0A0A0A on cream #F8F6F1. 18.3:1.

Reversed. White on ink. 19.8:1. Used on dark sections and the cover.

On accent. Ink on primary-500 — 6.6:1. Never white on orange.

On papaya. Ink on #FAEACD — the hero glow tint. 16.7:1.

The grey variant

#545454

as shipped in public/logo.svg

The standalone SVG ships mid-grey so it sits at the same weight as the grayscale client logos in a logo wall. Use it only in peer contexts — partner strips, directory listings, footer credit rows. Anywhere the brand speaks for itself, use ink or white.

On photography and shaders

The mark has no built-in container, so it needs the background to do the work. Over a shader gradient or an image, place it on an area that is uniformly light or uniformly dark and clears 4.5:1 against the fill you chose.

If you can't find that area, don't add a scrim behind the mark alone — move the mark, or place it on a solid band that runs the full width of the composition.

arcaniStudio — Brand Guidelines 07 · Misuse

Six ways to break it

The geometry is the identity. Every one of these makes it read as a different mark.

✕

Don't rotate. The pinwheel already implies motion. Turning it just looks like a mistake.

✕

Don't stretch. Scale proportionally, always. The artboard is square and stays square.

✕

Don't recolour. Ink, white, or the single grey. Gradients are for backgrounds, never for the mark.

✕

Don't outline. It's a filled path. An outlined version loses the solid-to-void relationship entirely.

✕

Don't add effects. No shadow, bevel, glow, or 3D extrude. Flat, always.

✕

Don't box it. No holding shape, badge, or plate. Clear space replaces the container.

Also: don't redraw it by hand, don't trace it from a screenshot, don't use it as a repeating pattern tile at small scale, and don't set it in a sentence as a letterform substitute.

One live issue worth fixing

White on primary-600 measures 3.57:1. That clears WCAG AA for large text (≥ 24 px, or ≥ 18.66 px bold) but not for normal-size text.

The hero CTA sets its label at 16 px on primary-600 — so it currently fails AA at that size.

Two ways out: raise the label to 24 px, or move the fill to primary-700 (5.19:1), which passes at any size and barely shifts the look.

arcaniStudio — Brand Guidelines 10 · Colour in use

Five percent orange

The accent earns attention by being rare. If two things on a screen are orange, one of them is wrong.

70% surface
25% ink & grey
5%

Rough target for any full page or deck. The dark share rises on dark sections; the orange share does not.

✓ Do

One primary CTA per view, in primary-600. Everything else is a text link or a ghost button.

✓ Do

Use the serif accent word in primary-700 to carry the emphasis in a headline — one word or one line, not both halves.

✓ Do

Let cream and ink alternate in long scrolls. Transitions between them are compositional decisions — fade or hard cut, chosen on purpose.

✕ Don't

Set body copy, long headings, or nav links in orange. On cream, primary-500 is 2.78:1 — unreadable by AA standards.

✕ Don't

Introduce a second accent hue for a "secondary" action. Secondary means less weight, not a different colour.

✕ Don't

Use orange gradient text, orange drop shadows, or orange section backgrounds behind body copy.

On cream

Ink #0A0A0A headings · palette-600 body · palette-200 hairlines · white cards · orange accent.

On ink

White headings · palette-400 body · white/15 hairlines · palette-900 cards · primary-400 accent.

Selection

Global rule: selected text sits on primary-500 in white. Set once in ::selection; don't override per component.

Setting rules

  • Tighten as you scale up. Headlines carry negative tracking (about −0.02 em); body copy stays at default.
  • Measure. 60–75 characters per line for body. The hero column caps at max-w-3xl for exactly this reason.
  • One accent per headline. The serif takes one line or one phrase, never both halves.
  • No all-caps paragraphs. Uppercase is for micro-labels at 8–14 px with generous tracking, nothing longer.
  • Numbers align. Tabular figures in tables, pricing, and stats.

arcaniStudio — Brand Guidelines 14 · Layout & spacing

Space is the decoration

Twelve columns, a 4 px rhythm, hairline rules. Nothing else is needed to build hierarchy.

Grid — 12 columns

Content columns run asymmetric by default: 7/5, 8/4, 5/7. A 6/6 split is the exception, not the starting point. Reading columns cap at max-w-3xl; full-bleed is reserved for dark sections and shader backgrounds.

Borders and rules

One weight: 1 px. palette-200 on cream, white/15 on ink. Rules divide, they don't decorate — no side-stripe accent borders, no coloured left rails on cards.

Shadows are effectively unused. Depth comes from a change of surface — white card on cream — not from a blur.

arcaniStudio — Brand Guidelines 15 · Motion

Motion that answers

Every movement responds to something the visitor did — a hover, a scroll, a submit. Nothing loops on its own except the ambient shader.

Roll-up label

The signature interaction. On hover, the button label slides up out of frame while a duplicate slides in from below — 500 ms, both halves in sync. Used on the primary CTA.

Chevron exit / enter

The icon leaves right and re-enters from the left over the same 500 ms. Direction always follows the action's meaning — forward is right.

Scroll easing

Lenis owns scroll easing on the homepage; native scroll-behavior is deliberately set to auto so anchor jumps aren't double-eased.

Timings

InteractionDurationCurve
Hover / colour change150–200 msease-out
Roll-up label, chevron500 msease-in-out
Scroll reveal400–600 msease-out, staggered
Ambient shadercontinuousbackground only, never behind body copy

Reduced motion is not optional

Every animation, transition, scroll effect and shader must check prefers-reduced-motion: reduce and degrade to a static state — not a faster version of the same movement.

That includes Lenis, the wormhole shader, the counters, and the scroll-reveal text. If a component can't be made static, it doesn't ship.

Do

Move one property at a time. Opacity and transform only — they're the cheap ones.

Do

Stagger a group by 40–80 ms so a list reads as a sequence, not a flash.

Don't

Animate on load, bounce, parallax the type, or auto-advance a carousel.

arcaniStudio — Brand Guidelines 16 · Imagery

Show the work

We're judged on craft, so the imagery is the craft: real screens, real client marks, generated gradients. Never a stock photo of people pointing at a laptop.

Client logos

Every client mark ships grayscale WebP, matched on optical height rather than bounding box — a wide wordmark and a square mark are balanced by eye, not by pixels. Colour returns only inside a case study, never in the logo wall.

Screens and case studies

Product shots over people shots. Crop to the part of the interface that carries the point, on a cream or ink field with generous margin. No laptop mockups on wooden desks, no floating perspective phones, no drop shadows faking depth.

Photography, when it's unavoidable

Warm, natural light. Real workspace, real people, no eye contact with the lens. If the only option is stock, use a gradient or a screen instead.

Generated backgrounds

The shader gradient draws from the accent set — chocolate-cosmos, carmine, spanish-orange, selective-yellow, with ai-accent reserved for AI-related surfaces. It sits behind headlines and empty space only, at low enough intensity that any text over it still clears 4.5:1.

File standards

FormatWebP first; SVG for any mark or diagram
Portfolio shots1600 px wide, quality ~80
Client logosGrayscale WebP, ~400 px wide
OG image1200 × 630, mark + one line of copy on ink
Alt textAlways. Describe the content, not the file.

arcaniStudio — Brand Guidelines 18 · What we avoid

The anti-references

A brand is defined as much by what it refuses. These are the defaults every template falls into — and the reason clients can't tell two agencies apart.

The generic SaaS template look

✕

Gradient text on headlines.

✕

Hero metric blocks — "10k+ users · 99.9% uptime · 24/7 support" — with no source behind the numbers.

✕

A tiny uppercase eyebrow above every single section heading.

✕

Glassmorphism, neon glow borders, and floating blurred blobs behind everything.

Corporate agency clichés

✕

Stock photography of diverse teams high-fiving around a laptop.

✕

Identical three-card icon grids where the icons carry no information.

✕

01 / 02 / 03 numbering scaffolded onto every section whether it's a sequence or not.

✕

Coloured side-stripe accent borders down the left edge of cards and quotes.

The test before anything ships: could a competitor swap their logo onto this and have it still work? If yes, it isn't finished — go back and put something specific in it.
