The brand

Warm, editorial, crafted. Confident and premium, never corporate. Everything we publish should feel considered — like someone made a decision rather than picked a template.

The test before anything ships: could a competitor swap their logo onto this and have it still work? If yes, it isn't finished.

Colour Palette

Only the colours listed in the arcaniStudio brand guidelines may be used in the visual style. Others that are not listed are prohibited.

Brand Orange is the single accent — it carries roughly five percent of any layout. Deep Orange is its readable counterpart, used for text and solid buttons where white sits on top.

Depending on the type of material, you can opt for a light or a dark mode. For the dark mode, Ink is used in the background; for the light mode, Cream.

Typeface

Our main font is Switzer (Designed by Indian Type Foundry). We use it wherever possible. Switzer is a neutral grotesk with a wide variable range that stays legible at caption sizes and holds its character in display settings.

Reckless Neue is the accent serif — one phrase per layout, never body copy. If neither font is available, use any system sans-serif.
